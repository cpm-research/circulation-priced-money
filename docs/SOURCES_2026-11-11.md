survey), https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/-/media/project/clevelandfedtenant/fsbsite/reports/2026/2026-report-on-employer-firms/2026-report-on-employer-firms.pdf

Kansas City Fed survey: https://www.kansascityfed.org/documents/15405/Small-Business-Lending-Survey-Kansas-City-Fed-Fourth-Quarter-2025.pdf
JPMorgan Chase Institute: https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/all-topics/business-growth-and-entrepreneurship/report-cash-flows-balances-and-buffer-days

**Reading.** The business loan in the model (how often, how large, at what rate) matches the survey. The
two places where the model is kinder to firms than the data are access: every card holder has an unlimited
line, and every loan request is granted. Both hide need, so they most likely understate what a pool that
lends on receipts would do for firms; the 4 Nov note on card lines recorded the net direction as unknown.
